# Changelog

Notable changes to Greenroom Community Edition are recorded here. Versions
track `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`.

## v0.42.1

- 精简七个面试准备技能的发现描述，入口根据用户目标选择完整准备或单步任务。
- 完整流程与工作台初始化按需读取，直接复用已提供的材料，避免重复确认和自动扩大任务。
- 用户明确风格偏好优先于默认写作习惯；事实来源、隐私和工作台格式契约保持不变。

## v0.42.0 — 2026-08

The first standalone Greenroom Community Edition release.

- Seven interview-preparation Skills covering job research, story development,
  interview scripts, industry briefs, mock interviews, and debriefs.
- A public knowledge base with 163 role entries across 27 groups.
- A portable Markdown workspace contract, fictional examples, and compatible
  file-format tools.
- Product-grade English and Chinese project guides with clear installation,
  contribution, licensing, and interoperability documentation.

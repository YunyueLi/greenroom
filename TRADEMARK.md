# greenroom Trademark and Brand Policy

The greenroom name, logo, wordmark, app icon, domain names, product screenshots,
visual identity, and distinctive product look and feel are reserved by Yunyue
Li.

## Allowed Uses

You may:

- refer to the open-core project as "greenroom core"
- state that a tool is "compatible with greenroom core" if it implements the
  public workspace contract
- link to the official project or documentation
- use the name in factual, non-misleading technical discussion

## Restricted Uses

You may not, without written permission:

- use the greenroom name, logo, icon, or wordmark for a competing product or
  hosted service
- imply that your project is official, endorsed, or operated by greenroom
- use greenroom brand assets in app icons, marketing pages, screenshots, or
  store listings
- register confusingly similar domains, packages, products, or social accounts
- copy the official product visual identity in a way that confuses users

## Forks and Compatible Tools

Forks and compatible tools should use their own names and visual identity. A
clear phrase such as "compatible with greenroom core" is acceptable; presenting
the fork as "greenroom" is not.

For brand permission, contact the project owner.
